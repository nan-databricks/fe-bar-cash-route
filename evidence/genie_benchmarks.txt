Q4. Which districts have the highest cash-out risk?

SQL:
WITH district_risk AS (
  SELECT
    `gold_cash_nodes`.`district` AS `district`,
    COUNT(*) AS `at_risk_nodes`,
    SUM(`gold_node_status`.`current_cash_thb`) AS `cash_thb`
  FROM `stable_classic_nan_fe_vm_catalog`.`snb_cash_ops`.`gold_node_status` AS `gold_node_status`
  JOIN `stable_classic_nan_fe_vm_catalog`.`snb_cash_ops`.`gold_cash_nodes` AS `gold_cash_nodes`
    ON `gold_node_status`.`node_id` = `gold_cash_nodes`.`node_id`
  WHERE `gold_cash_nodes`.`district` IS NOT NULL
    AND `gold_node_status`.`avg_daily_outflow_28d` > 0
    AND try_divide(`gold_node_status`.`current_cash_thb`,`gold_node_status`.`avg_daily_outflow_28d`) <= 3
  GROUP BY `gold_cash_nodes`.`district`
)
SELECT
  `district`,
  `at_risk_nodes`,
  `cash_thb`,
  RANK() OVER (ORDER BY `at_risk_nodes` DESC) AS `risk_rank`
FROM district_risk
ORDER BY `risk_rank`, `district`

ANSWER:
You're looking for a ranked list of districts showing which ones have the highest risk of cash running out soon. The query counts how many nodes in each district are at risk of cash-out within 3 days and sums the current cash held at those nodes.

As of **2026-09-30** at the **district** level, **Phon** has the highest cash-out risk with **11** at-risk nodes (about **THB 1,038,000** current cash across those nodes). Examples include:
- **Phon** — **11** at-risk nodes, about **THB 1,038,000**

- **Mueang Khon Kaen** — **9** at-risk nodes, about **THB 1,005,000**
- **Ban Phai** — **7** at-risk nodes, about **THB 776,000**
- **Mancha Khiri** — **7** at-risk nodes, about **THB 889,000**
Across the **7 districts** returned, the risk count ranges from **6 to 11** at-risk nodes, with **Ban Haet** carrying the highest cash balance among the higher-risk districts shown.
