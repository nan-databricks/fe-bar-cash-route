Q4. Which districts have the highest cash-out risk?

WITH district_risk AS (
  SELECT
    `gold_cash_nodes`.`district` AS `district`,
    COUNT(*) AS `at_risk_nodes`,
    SUM(`gold_node_status`.`current_cash_thb`) AS `cash_thb`
  FROM `stable_classic_nan_fe_vm_catalog`.`snb_cash_ops`.`gold_node_status` AS `gold_node_status`
  JOIN `stable_classic_nan_fe_vm_catalog`.`snb_cash_ops`.`gold_cash_nodes` AS `gold_cash_nodes`
    ON `gold_node_status`.`node_id` = `gold_cash_nodes`.`node_id`
  WHERE `gold_node_status`.`avg_daily_outflow_28d` > 0
    AND try_divide(`gold_node_status`.`current_cash_thb`,`gold_node_status`.`avg_daily_outflow_28d`) <= 3
    AND `gold_cash_nodes`.`district` IS NOT NULL
  GROUP BY `gold_cash_nodes`.`district`
)
SELECT
  `district`,
  `at_risk_nodes`,
  `cash_thb`,
  RANK() OVER (ORDER BY `at_risk_nodes` DESC) AS `risk_rank`
FROM district_risk
ORDER BY `risk_rank`, `district`

ANSWER:
You're looking for a ranked list of districts based on cash-out risk as of the snapshot date 2026-09-30. The query counts the number of nodes in each district that are at risk of cashing out within 3 days, considering only nodes that have a positive average daily cash outflow. It also sums the current cash held at these at-risk nodes per district. The results are ordered by the number of at-risk nodes, showing the highest risk districts first.

As of **2026-09-30**, at the **district** level, **Phon** has the highest cash-out risk with **11** at-risk nodes within **3 days** and about **THB 1.038 million** in cash across those nodes. Examples include:
- **Phon** — **11** at-risk nodes, **THB 1.038 million**
- **Ban Haet** — **9** at-risk nodes, **THB 1.749 million**
- **Mueang Khon Kaen** — **9** at-risk nodes, **THB 1.005 million**
- **Ban Phai** — **7** at-risk nodes, **THB 0.776 million**
- **Mancha Khiri** — **7** at-risk nodes, **THB 0.889 million**

Across the **7 districts** returned, the risk count ranges from **6 to 11** at-risk nodes, with **Ban Haet** holding the largest cash balance among the high-risk districts shown.
