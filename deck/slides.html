The problem

A calendar, not demand, decides where cash goes

SNB replenishes 159 branches, 400 ATMs and 120 cash-recycling machines from 3 cash centres with 30 armoured vans, every 5 days, whatever the demand.

1

Wasted stops

Vans visit machines that are still mostly full because "it's day 5".

Fuel · crew hours · wear
2

Cash-outs

Payday and holiday spikes empty ATMs before the next visit.

Lost fees · complaints · trust
3

Idle cash

Cassettes and vaults overstocked "just in case".

Non-earning float
4

Static routes

Same sequence every cycle; overruns opening windows.

Excess km · overtime
Cash Ops plans from yesterday's spreadsheets and cannot answer: "Which machines run dry by Friday, and which van should go?"

Lakeflow + Unity Catalog

Trusted cash data, governed once

Lakeflow Spark Declarative Pipelines · snb_cash_ops_pipeline
  • Bronze: Auto Loader ingests 8 feeds (depots, vans, branches, machines, calendar, daily machine and branch cash, CIT trips) incrementally
  • Silver: typed tables with expectations: drop null keys and negative cash; warn on coordinates outside Thailand
  • Gold: unified node cash flow, demand features, node status and baseline KPIs as materialized views
  • Event log and data-quality metrics captured for every update
Unity Catalog governance
  • Column mask on CIT custodian name and phone for anyone outside snb_pii_readers
  • Row filter by depot: a dispatcher in one cash centre sees only that centre's trips
  • Tags for layer, domain and PII; gold-only grants to consumers
  • Lineage from raw file to the app, visible in Catalog Explorer
Why Databricks: quality rules, security and lineage are declared once and enforced for SQL, ML, Genie and the App alike. No copies, no second security model.

What we chose, and what it costs

DecisionWhyTrade-off
Declarative pipeline, not notebooksExpectations, lineage, incremental by defaultLess ad-hoc flexibility
One global LightGBM, not 679 modelsShares signal, handles new ATMs, one endpointLess per-node tuning
P80 quantile for replenishmentA cash-out costs more than a little extra cashSome extra float carried
OR-Tools, 25 s per depotOpen source, explainable, fastNot provably optimal
Lakebase synced tables for the AppFast reads plus writable overridesSnapshot freshness, not streaming
Genie agent mode, not a custom agentGoverned by UC, no prompt plumbing, fast to valueLess custom tool control

Path to production

Pilot one cash centre, then scale and harden

Phase 1 · 30 CIT days

Pilot

Khon Kaen Central cash centre; real ATM switch, vault and CIT feeds under the same silver contract.

Exit: forecast beats current planner; ≥ 80% of planned stops accepted
Phase 2

Scale

All 3 cash centres; nightly plan plus a mid-morning re-forecast on payday.

Exit: cost per day and cash-out KPIs tracked weekly
Phase 3

Harden

IdP groups for row filters, maker-checker on overrides, CI/CD for the bundle, model monitoring, road-network travel times.

Exit: Risk and Security sign-off; PDPA review
Phase 4

Extend

Denomination mix, vault planning, CIT vendor SLAs, other regions.

Exit: region-by-region rollout
Humans confirm every plan; nothing moves cash autonomously. Core banking, CIT vendors and radio dispatch stay in place.
