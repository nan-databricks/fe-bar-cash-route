Root causes

Why it happens

1 · Fragmented data

ATM switch, vault system, CIT trip logs, core banking and holiday spreadsheets live apart.

2 · Know-how stuck with people

Drivers and dispatchers know which sites are closed or busy; it travels by radio.

3 · No forecast

A calendar rule decides the visit, not expected withdrawals.

4 · Day-old manual planning

Routes are built in Excel from yesterday's numbers.

5 · No feedback loop or audit

Overrides happen by phone; nobody can see why a stop changed.

6 · No common security model

Custodian PII and depot access are controlled differently in every system.

“Data, forecast and people's know-how never meet in one place.”

Appendix

Appendix: decisions and trade-offs

DecisionWhyTrade-off
Declarative pipeline, not notebooksExpectations, lineage, incremental by defaultLess ad-hoc flexibility
One global LightGBM, not 679 modelsShares signal, handles new ATMs, one endpointLess per-node tuning
P80 quantile for replenishmentA cash-out costs more than a little extra cashSome extra float carried
OR-Tools, 25 s per depotOpen source, explainable, fastNot provably optimal
Lakebase synced tables for the AppFast reads plus writable overridesSnapshot freshness, not streaming
Genie agent mode, not a custom agentGoverned by Unity Catalog, no prompt plumbing, fast to valueLess custom tool control
Serverless everywhereNo cluster ops, scale to zero for a once-a-day planCold starts
